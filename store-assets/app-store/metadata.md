# App Store metadata — Brote

## English (U.S.)

### Promotional Text (140/170)

```
Track any number that matters: savings, km, books or kilos. Log in seconds, watch your ring fill up and keep your goals on your Home Screen.
```

### Description

```
Brote turns any goal into a ring you can watch fill up.

Save for a trip, run 100 km, read 24 books, lose weight or pay off a debt. If it can be counted, Brote can track it.

GOALS THAT GO UP OR DOWN
Some goals grow (savings, km, pages) and some shrink (weight, debt, screen time). Brote handles both, and your progress always moves toward 100%.

START WHERE YOU ARE
Already halfway there? Set your starting value and current progress, and Brote picks up from that point.

LOG IN SECONDS
Tap +1, +5, +10 or your usual amount, add a note if you want, and you're done. You can also enter the new total directly.

SEE YOUR PROGRESS GROW
Each goal shows your last 8 weeks as a trend line, your weekly pace, your streak and your full history.

ON YOUR HOME SCREEN
Widgets show one goal's ring or a list of up to five goals, and StandBy shows up to four at a glance.

GENTLE REMINDERS
Choose the days and time for a nudge to log your progress.

YOUR DATA, YOUR PHONE
Your goals are saved on your phone and you can use Brote without an account. Turn on cloud backup to keep your goals safe when you change phones, and export everything to CSV whenever you like.

BROTE PRO
Try Pro free for 7 days. After that, unlock it with a single payment: no subscription and no monthly charges.
• Unlimited goals
• Widgets and StandBy
• Cloud backup
• Reminders

Available in English and Spanish.
```
